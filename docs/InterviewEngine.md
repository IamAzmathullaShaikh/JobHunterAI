# Interview Intelligence Engine

The Interview Engine prepares you for the final hurdle of the job search.

## 1. Question Generation
- **Behavioral**: Generates STAR-method questions based on the job's cultural values.
- **Technical**: Deep-dives into the tech stack mentioned in the JD.
- **JD-Specific**: Finds specific requirements in the JD and asks "How have you handled X in the past?"

## 2. Prep Studio
- **Mock Interviews**: Interactive AI-driven chat where the AI acts as the interviewer.
- **Feedback Loop**: After each answer, the AI provides:
    - **Score**: How well you answered.
    - **Improvements**: Specific suggestions on how to improve your answer.
    - **Sample Answer**: A "perfect" response for comparison.

## 3. Future Roadmap: Voice Mock Studio
The next major update (v1.1.0) will include:
- **Speech-to-Text**: Allowing you to speak your answers.
- **Tone Analysis**: Detecting confidence and clarity in your voice.
- **Zero-Latency Response**: Using local audio processing.
