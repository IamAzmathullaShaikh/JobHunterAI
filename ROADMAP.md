# JobHunterAI Roadmap

Our goal is to build the ultimate autonomous career assistant.

## Q3 2026: v1.1.0 - The Interactive Era
- [ ] **Voice Prep Studio**: Real-time mock interviews using WebSpeech API and local STT.
- [ ] **Recruiter Outreach v2**: Automated LinkedIn connection requests and follow-ups.
- [ ] **Email Integration**: Sync your job-related emails directly into the CRM.

## Q4 2026: v1.2.0 - The Browser Era
- [ ] **Chrome Extension**: One-click "Apply & Track" from any job site.
- [ ] **PDF Autofill**: Automatically fill out standard job application forms using your profile data.
- [ ] **Multi-Profile Support**: Manage different resumes for different career paths.

## 2027: v2.0.0 - Fully Autonomous Search
- [ ] **Autonomous Agent**: An AI agent that searches, matches, tailors, and applies for jobs while you sleep.
- [ ] **Salary Negotiation Bot**: Provides real-time guidance during offer negotiations.
- [ ] **Career Path Prediction**: Predictive analytics on which roles will lead to your long-term career goals.
